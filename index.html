About the Firm

Built on Decades of Investment Experience

ID Telecom Partners brings together private equity discipline and family office capital to back exceptional businesses at inflection points — where the right capital and the right partner make the difference.

We operate with a long-duration capital base, allowing us to be patient, committed owners rather than short-cycle fund managers. Our investment horizon aligns with the growth trajectory of our companies, not with an arbitrary fund expiration date.

Our team draws on decades of operational, deal-making, and capital markets experience — giving us conviction where others see complexity and the ability to move with speed when the right opportunity presents itself.

Family Office Capital

Permanent, patient capital from a single family office — no fund cycles, no LP pressure, no forced exits.

Operational Expertise

We've built, operated, and scaled businesses across multiple industries. We bring hands-on operational knowledge, not just check-writing.

Regulatory Acumen

Navigating complex regulatory, operational, and market dynamics is a core competency — we lean into complexity where others step back.

Network of Relationships

Decades of relationships with operators, founders, capital markets participants, and industry veterans accelerate our diligence and deal flow.
